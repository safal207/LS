# LS Project One-Pager (Grant/Foundation)

## Project

**LS (GhostGPT / Local Cognitive System)** is a local-first AI coordination and oversight runtime designed to make human-plus-model operations safer, more auditable, and more reproducible.

## Problem

Many applied AI systems fail in production due to:

- weak observability of model behavior,
- poor reproducibility across environments,
- insufficient safety and quality governance,
- fragmented evidence for external reviewers and funders.

## Solution

LS combines runtime orchestration, quality gates, and safety-oriented operational workflows:

- local-first agent runtime and operator loop,
- CI quality gates and security checks,
- structured evaluation and artifact-based reporting,
- grant-facing governance, roadmap, and KPI framework.

## Why It Matters

LS targets the gap between "demo AI" and "deployable, reviewable AI systems" by providing:

- measurable reliability improvements,
- explicit risk handling and remediation process,
- reproducible evidence for independent assessment.

## Current State

- active CI/security workflows,
- documented architecture and safety strategy,
- grant-readiness package with roadmap, threat model, governance, and metrics.

## 12-Month Objectives

1. Stabilize reliability and reproducibility (green-main discipline, deterministic demo path).
2. Improve security posture (faster remediation and clearer policy enforcement).
3. Publish evidence artifacts (case studies, KPI trend reports, reviewer-ready bundles).
