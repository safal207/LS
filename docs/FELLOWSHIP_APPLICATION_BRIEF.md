# Fellowship Application Brief

## One-line summary

LS is a local-first coordination and oversight runtime for human-plus-model systems.

## Short project description

This repository focuses on turning multi-model and multi-signal reasoning into
reviewable, replayable, and measurable coordination loops. Instead of treating
 model outputs as disposable text, LS records council cycles, tracks contribution,
 measures receiver resonance, supports human approval, and exposes replayable
 traces for debugging and governance.

## Why this matters

Many agentic systems fail in opaque ways:

- they produce outputs without clear adoption logic
- they do not track which model actually improved the final result
- they lack strong human approval and audit pathways
- they are difficult to replay, evaluate, or compare over time

LS addresses this by combining:

- council-cycle artifacts
- contribution and reputation tracking
- receiver-resonance measurement
- quality gates and structured test intelligence
- replayable traces through LTP

## Core repository claims

This repository already demonstrates:

1. human-in-the-loop approval-safe workflows
2. council-cycle ledgers for model participation and adoption
3. contribution, reputation, and merit updates grounded in real cycles
4. replay and inspection for task and coordination traces
5. machine-readable quality reports and CI enforcement

## Research angle

The research direction is not generic assistant UX.

The stronger framing is:

- measurable receiver resonance
- replayable safety and evaluation artifacts
