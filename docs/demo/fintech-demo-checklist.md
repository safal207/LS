# Fintech Demo Checklist (3–5 Minutes)

## What to open first

1. `docs/demo/fintech-release-scenario.md` (scenario framing).
2. `docs/demo/fintech-release-sample-data.md` (structured input sketch).
3. `docs/demo/fintech-release-expected-output.md` (layer-by-layer advisory excerpts).

## What to say in the first 30 seconds

- “This is one compliance-heavy fintech release with real deadline pressure.”
- “Four parties are right for different reasons; the issue is coordination, not a single missing task.”
- “LS provides advisory interpretation, not autonomous approval or policy execution.”

## What output to keep on screen

Keep the final `coordination_advisory_summary` excerpt visible while explaining how it was derived from the previous layers.

## 3 takeaways that must land

1. LS compresses cross-functional ambiguity into bounded advisory fields.
2. The advisory is traceable to intermediate layers (not black-box prose).
3. The output supports faster, clearer review discussion without replacing human signoff.

## Anti-patterns to avoid

- Do not show multiple scenarios in one short demo.
- Do not present LS as an auto go/no-go authority.
- Do not drown the audience in full-object JSON dumps.
- Do not skip the tension explanation before showing final summary.
