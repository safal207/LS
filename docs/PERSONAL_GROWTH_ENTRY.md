# Personal Growth Entry Point

This page is a short reviewer-facing entry point for the Personal Cognitive Garden direction in LS.

## One-sentence positioning

LS turns AI sessions into a human-owned, goal-directed cognitive garden: a graph of goals, skills, decisions, constraints, evidence, reflections, and growth paths that agents help cultivate under governance.

## What changed in the framing

LS should not be explained as a generic cognitive network.

It is better explained as a **personal development infrastructure layer**:

```text
human-owned graph
+ goal direction
+ agent contribution signals
+ governance and consent
+ replayable evidence
= personal cognitive garden
```

## Why this is useful

Most AI products produce answers. The answers often disappear into chat history.

LS asks a stronger question:

> What should this interaction add to the person's long-term development graph?

That graph can support:

- personal growth and self-direction;
- founder and executive coaching;
- engineering and QA development;
- learning paths;
- onboarding;
- team capability development;
- safer agent memory and profile writes;
- evidence-backed progress reviews.

## Safe language

Use this language:

- personal cognitive garden;
- human-owned graph;
- goal-directed development;
- agents as contributors;
- proposed updates;
- human/governance authorization;
- replayable evidence;
- continuity with consent.

Avoid this language:

- global brain;
- shared consciousness;
- AI knows the person better than themselves;
- automatic identity updates;
- employer access to private growth memory;
- psychological truth claims.

## Product wedge

Core commercial line:

> Every AI session should compound into human development.

Possible first products:

- personal growth graph;
- weekly AI-assisted development review;
- founder development cockpit;
- employee onboarding growth graph;
- AI coaching with evidence and consent;
- manager-safe development summaries;
- agent-memory governance layer.

## Boundary

The person owns the garden.

Agents may suggest updates. Governance decides what becomes durable state.

Company deployments should separate private personal memory from aggregated or explicitly shared development signals.
