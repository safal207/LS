# Investor Mini Deck (5 Slides After Hero)

## Purpose

After the hero opening slide, use a compact 5-slide sequence before building a full deck.

## Slide 1 — Demo proof (3–5 minute flow)

Show:
- canonical fintech scenario,
- layer chain (`alignment -> stabilization -> snapshot -> playbook advisory -> summary`),
- final bounded advisory output.

Audience takeaway:
- LS turns fragmented coordination evidence into a review-ready advisory verdict.

## Slide 2 — Wedge (where to win first)

Show:
- first segment: fintech / compliance-heavy release decisions,
- buyer roles: product ops, release/program lead, risk/governance owner,
- pain: delay, review churn, and incident risk from mis-coordination.

## Slide 3 — Why now

Show current shift:
- more cross-functional AI-assisted workflows,
- higher governance and audit pressure,
- generic agents commoditizing.

Point:
- market now values explainable domain-specific decision layers.

## Slide 4 — Moat and defensibility

Show defensibility elements:
- deterministic schema,
- contract-tested outputs,
- advisory traceability,
- reusable scenario packs,
- workflow embedding in review/governance processes.

## Slide 5 — Expansion path

Show growth arc:
- start with release coordination in regulated teams,
- expand into adjacent high-stakes coordination workflows,
- become a standard interpretation layer between signal and execution.

## Presenter note

Keep the tone dense and factual. Avoid hype and autonomous-execution framing.
