# Investor Hero Opening Slide (Text + Visual Layout)

## Purpose

Create one strong opening slide for investor conversations:
- expensive problem,
- clear promise,
- why now,
- why LS,
- why this can become a market.

> This is a **content and layout spec**, not a shipped product UI.

## Slide headline options

### English (default)

**Explainable coordination verdicts for high-stakes teams**

Subheadline:

**LS helps fintech and compliance-heavy teams decide whether the current strategy fits the live coordination scene — before execution, incidents, or review churn.**

### Russian (optional localized version)

**Проблема не в нехватке данных. Проблема в том, что никто не даёт объяснимый вердикт: подходит ли текущая стратегия живой ситуации.**

Подзаголовок:

**LS — explainable coordination advisory stack, который превращает сигналы из product / ops / compliance / security в компактный review-ready advisory output.**

## Core visual layout (single slide)

### Top band
- Headline
- Subheadline

### Middle: 2-column contrast

#### Left — "What breaks today" (Before)
- Dashboards show activity, not strategy-to-scene fit.
- Reviews reconcile fragmented evidence instead of deciding.
- Generic agents summarize text, but do not emit bounded review-ready advisory outputs.

#### Right — "What LS adds" (After)
Show one advisory card with contract-aligned fields:

```yaml
coordination_advisory_summary:
  coordination_advisory_label: fragile
  coordination_readiness: 0.59
  primary_intervention_mode: stabilization_first
  playbook_support_level: medium
  top_risk_driver: coordination_risk
  summary_reason: scene is fragile: coordination risk is elevated and playbook grounding is limited
```

Caption:

**From fragmented signals to an explainable coordination verdict.**

### Bottom band: wedge + moat
- **First wedge:** fintech / compliance-heavy release decisions.
- **Early moat:** deterministic schema, contract-tested outputs, advisory traceability, reusable scenario library.

## 6 investor questions this slide must answer

1. What is the expensive problem?
2. Why do old tools fail on this problem?
3. What exactly does LS add?
4. Why now?
5. Why is this not "just another AI agent"?
6. Why can this become a larger market layer?

## Style rules (ClickFunnels mechanics, investor-safe)

Use:
- clarity,
- high signal headline,
- expensive pain,
- concrete transformation,
- simple visual logic.

Avoid:
- hype claims,
- sci-fi UI,
- "#1 in the world" language,
- autonomous-policy-execution framing.
